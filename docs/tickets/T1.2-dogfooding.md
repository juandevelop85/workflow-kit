# T1.2 · The kit applies its own workflow

> **Status:** ✅ Done
> **Phase:** Phase 1 (credibility)
> **Priority:** High
> **Date:** 2026-11-01
> **Depends on:** T1.1
> **Origin:** audit ("the kit that preaches traceability is not traceable")

## Description

The kit had no `.agents/`, no backlog and no tickets of its own; its commits referenced a ticket of
another project. A method that its own authors do not follow is not credible, and its friction is
never felt by the people who could remove it.

## Technical Scope

1. Project layer of the kit in `.agents/` (config, rules, threat model, baseline); the core directories
   are symlinks to `core/` so changes are used immediately. `.claude/` links to `.agents/`.
2. A backlog with this phase's tickets; `CLAUDE.md` and `AGENTS.md`.
3. A test that runs the kit's own checker over the kit.
4. Out of scope: changing the checker's rules (T1.4, T1.5).

## Acceptance Criteria

- The kit passes its own checker, including the format of its recent commits. test: test_the_kit_passes_its_own_checker
- `.agents/` links the core instead of copying it, and `.claude/` links `.agents/`. test: test_the_core_is_linked_not_copied
- The kit's gates are declared and pass. inspection: `python3 .agents/scripts/workflow_check.py gates --run` prints `0 failed`.

## Risks

A symlinked core means a broken change breaks the next session too: that is the point (it is felt).
